# Attaching a file or a transcript to a capture

Capture takes a title and nothing else. The cases that strain that: a failing test's full output, a pasted
conversation that explains why something matters, a screenshot. Today all of it goes in the body as pasted text,
which works and is ugly for anything long.

Whether this needs an affordance at all is a question for the first real week of use rather than for now — the
honest answer might be that pasting into the body is fine forever, since the backlog item is read once and then
triaged.

Nobody has committed to it. Raised while removing the capability files' open-question sections.
